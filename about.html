About

DJ-Thïmble

DJ-Thïmble is an American metal DJ operating out of Tokyo, Japan. The name traces back to a band called Thimble, put together years ago on the West Coast of the United States. The music has changed form a few times since then. The commitment to heavy has not.

Relocating to Japan in 2011 meant trading one music scene for another. The Japanese metal underground is serious, dedicated, and genuinely loud. It made sense to get involved.

The sets run across the harder end of the metal spectrum: death, black, thrash, doom, and anything else with enough weight behind it. No pop metal, no crowd-pleasing crossover. Just the real thing, played at volume, for people who know the difference.

The umlaut is intentional. So is everything else.
